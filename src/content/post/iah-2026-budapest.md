Katie is heading to Budapest this September for the [IAH World Groundwater Congress 2026](https://iah2026budapest.com/), with a pre-congress short course and a talk.

**Pre-congress short course: Advanced Groundwater Modeling Techniques with MODFLOW 6 and PEST++**

A two-day hands-on course running **September 12–13, 2026** at the ELTE Lágymányos Campus, co-taught with Joseph Hughes, Mike Fienen, Rui Hugman, and Jeremy White.

- **Day 1** covers the latest functionality in MODFLOW 6 and PEST++, with FloPy and pyEMU scripting exercises
- **Day 2** moves into uncertainty analysis, ensemble methods, management optimization, and emulation techniques

More details and registration are on the [pre- and post-congress courses page](https://iah2026budapest.com/pre-and-post-congress-courses/).

**Talk: Exploring the promise of InSAR ground deformation in regional groundwater simulation**

Katie will also present in Session 4.3, *Advances in modeling groundwater dynamics from regional to global scales*, on **Wednesday, September 16, 16:30–16:45 (Room: Brahms)**.

If you're headed to IAH 2026, come say hi!
